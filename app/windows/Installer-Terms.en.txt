WoB Model Viewer — installation and licence
Copyright (c) 2026 RIGELIO Studio

By accepting this page you agree to install WoB Model Viewer for the current Windows account. Setup registers the viewer for .glb, .gltf and .wobview. You can choose the default app separately in Windows Settings. The viewer runs offline. Uninstall removes the installed program files, shortcuts, its file-type registration and the viewer's own settings, logs and caches. Your saved models, view files and screenshots belong to you and the app preserves them.
