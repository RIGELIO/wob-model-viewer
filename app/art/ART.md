## Icon prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. A new native desktop program icon for WoB Model Viewer by RIGELIO Studio. Square original art, one centered simple warm brass low-poly medieval bust in pronounced side profile transitioning into 4–5 clearly readable technical triangular wireframe lines, on a rounded square near-black graphite app tile. Tiny-size readability is the priority: large portrait silhouette, hard recognizable contours, very few edges, superior value contrast, quietly tactile brushed antique gold and soft steel-grey background. Saturated gold single highlight edge. Tile fills about 90% of image, with fully transparent short margin outside its rounded corners. No lettering, no tiny features, no watermarks, no extra badges or devices.

## Startup artwork prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Create an original premium BOOT SPLASH image for the Windows and Linux 3D asset inspection application WoB Model Viewer by RIGELIO Studio. Landscape 16:10 composition, use a near-solid dark graphite background #121315 across the outer 10% in all directions so the boot splash blends into the app clear color. Center a large original medallion, antique brass low-poly medieval Bohemian male bust in three-quarter view, left half a tactile metal sculpture, right half sparse luminous technical triangular wireframe, in the exact look of a premium desktop modeling tool. Thin selective brass orbit marks and barely visible mesh geometry are subordinate. Beneath the emblem, render precisely the title “WoB Model Viewer” in elegant readable warm ivory serif typography, and the smaller credit “RIGELIO STUDIO”. Strong legibility from far away, a restrained dark metal and antique gold palette. Keep the focus centered, spacious, historically inspired, no gods, no weapons, no fake loading bar, no OS window chrome, no watermarks, no other logos. One flattened landscape startup graphic.

## Desktop design prompt

> Use case: ui-mockup. Widescreen 16:10 desktop app mockup of WoB Model Viewer, a Godot tool for inspecting historical Bohemian character GLBs. Dark graphite window #10141c, subtle warm brass accents #edbd7c, cream typography. Design the wide immersive viewport and a focused right inspector. A three-quarter male head and shoulders with fine stubble is centered in the viewport. Top row has a small brass sculpted-head app icon, title 'WoB Model Viewer', file 'character_lod0.glb', button 'Open GLB', button 'Save 4K PNG', compact 'EN / CS'. Viewport toolbar 'Frame', 'Front', 'Side', 'Wireframe'. Right inspector sections 'Lighting', 'HDRI', 'Light intensity', 'Azimuth', 'Elevation', 'Background', 'Geometry', 'Materials & diagnostics'. Bottom 'Animation' picker, play button, scrubber. Small hint 'Left drag to orbit · Right drag to pan · Scroll to zoom'. Well-spaced controls, elegant solid surfaces and thin separators, refined professional desktop UX. No fake branding, no splash screen, no device framing, no watermark.
