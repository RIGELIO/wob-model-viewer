# WoB Model Viewer art

The app icon and design reference were created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The icon was resized to 256 × 256 RGBA and converted to a multi-resolution Windows ICO. The design reference informed the application layout and is kept as a design artifact.

## Icon prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished square desktop app icon for WoB Model Viewer, a medieval game studio's 3D character model inspection tool. One centered app tile with realistic brushed brass edges and a deep blue-black slate rounded square. Inside, a single three-quarter view of a generic medieval sculpted male head and shoulders, split into warm bronze shaded polygon surfaces on one side and a few bold gold geometric wireframe edges on the other. Strong readable silhouette at 24 pixels. Simple major planes, restrained highlights, muted gold and cool graphite. Tile occupies 86–90% of the square with transparent margin, centered straight-on. No letters, text, watermark, rings, or additional objects.

## Desktop design prompt

> Use case: ui-mockup. Widescreen 16:10 desktop app mockup of WoB Model Viewer, a Godot tool for inspecting historical Bohemian character GLBs. Dark graphite window #10141c, subtle warm brass accents #edbd7c, cream typography. Design the wide immersive viewport and a focused right inspector. A three-quarter male head and shoulders with fine stubble is centered in the viewport. Top row has a small brass sculpted-head app icon, title 'WoB Model Viewer', file 'character_lod0.glb', button 'Open GLB', button 'Save 4K PNG', compact 'EN / CS'. Viewport toolbar 'Frame', 'Front', 'Side', 'Wireframe'. Right inspector sections 'Lighting', 'HDRI', 'Light intensity', 'Azimuth', 'Elevation', 'Background', 'Geometry', 'Materials & diagnostics'. Bottom 'Animation' picker, play button, scrubber. Small hint 'Left drag to orbit · Right drag to pan · Scroll to zoom'. Well-spaced controls, elegant solid surfaces and thin separators, refined professional desktop UX. No fake branding, no splash screen, no device framing, no watermark.
